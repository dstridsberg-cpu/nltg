What you get

  • The page exactly as rendered at your current window width, with all styles built into one file.
  • Form fields as filled in, and sections open or closed as they were.
  • No scripts or trackers. It's a frozen picture you can edit, not a working page.
  • Images load from Ving's image server, unless you store them in the file.

Good to know

  • Buttons, accordions and dropdowns don't respond in the copy.
  • For a mobile copy, make the window narrow or use Chrome's device toolbar first.
  • Personal data removal catches what was typed into fields. Details pre-filled from a profile can remain, so check before sharing.
  • Tested in Chrome. Edge, Arc and Brave should behave the same. Safari and Firefox haven't been tested.
